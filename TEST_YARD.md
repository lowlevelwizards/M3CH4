# Test Yard — isolated full-catalogue training range

Open `test-yard.html` from the campaign's **TEST YARD** shortcut.

- **Bench:** All currently implemented `CATALOG` definitions, with real assembly graph validation, mass and power checks. No purchased inventory requirement.
- **Deploy:** Play using the normal cockpit, movement, recoil, projectile, reload, energy, hit-location and damage systems on a purpose-built 18m / 31m / 51m range.
- **Dummies:** Three stationary genuine mech hit volumes, with fresh armor and internals; no AI fire or objective timer. **LIVE AI:** let the enemies fight back; both modes support target rebuilding.
- **Reset rig:** restore player parts, ammunition, faults, and position. **Rebuild targets:** restore every target without redeploying.
- **Isolation:** Does not read/write machine, stable, economy, salvage, score, or campaign progress. The only persisted sandbox key is `m3ch4-test-yard-loadout-v1`. Leaving and returning to the main game keeps career saves untouched.
- **Mobile:** One compact toolbar and a collapsible scrollable bench; normal iPhone two-thumb controls once deployed.

Scope: test-yard inventory means every *implemented, fieldable* part in `CATALOG`, not future speculative designs from the long-term parts catalogue.
